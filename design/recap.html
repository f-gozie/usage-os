What the model did with more facts

Each day’s facts were sent to Apple’s on-device model twice, with instructions rewritten to ask for the day’s shape (same temperature as production). Marked: a name turned into a verb, a fact that isn’t in the facts, a dropped fact that matters, or a loop. B, asking the model only to smooth a draft the app wrote, is not shown: it handed the draft back word for word in 17 of 18 runs. It costs a second or two of battery each time and changes nothing.

DayModel output, run 1Run 2
FindingThe on-device model is a small one. With three or four facts it phrases them well enough; with ten, 9 of these 12 runs got something wrong: inventing times, saying “so far” about a finished day, moving a fact, or turning names into verbs. Shorter prompts didn’t fix it (“You were mostly Work”). So the richness has to come from the app, and the app should write the sentences too. The shipped recap has the verb problem too: an earlier run of today’s prompt wrote “mostly working for 4 hours 31 minutes”.

What I need from you

  1. C, the app writes the recap. The model stops being called for the recap. Alternative: keep the model on the short facts it handles today and add the shape only to the template fallback. That gives two recaps that say different things depending on whether the model happened to answer.
  2. What happens to the model code. My suggestion: leave the Swift helper and the eval in the repo but stop calling it, and record why. When Apple ships a better on-device model, rerun the same six days and switch it back on if it passes. The other choice is to delete it now and keep the project smaller.
  3. The badge. “≡ Template” makes the main recap sound like a placeholder. I’d drop the badge. Toggle it in the panel.
  4. Which facts, and their thresholds. Untick any family you don’t want, tune the sliders, then copy the values here.
  5. The words. “went to”, “counting 1h 9m just after midnight”, “It came in short pieces”, “The longest break was…”. Read them aloud on the six days and flag any that sound off.
